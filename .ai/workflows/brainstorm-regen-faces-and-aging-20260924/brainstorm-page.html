Touchline · brainstorm · 24 September 2026

Regen faces that look real and age

We started from one wish: the youth players that Touchline creates each season should have faces more realistic than Football Manager's, and those faces should age. Research came first. No football game ages a generated face. The games that age faces well store a face as parameters on a head. Most photoreal AI face tools carry non-commercial licences.

We now believe in one route. Each regen is a face genome with an identity that can age from day one, on a FLAME head with MakeHuman age and ancestry targets. Hair is hair cards, with dedicated coiled styles and 150 or more styles. Studio AI makes textures that ship as compact data. A Rust portrait process renders faces: followed players are baked at age-band edges, and everyone else is drawn on demand. Our best render is the default, a shader gives a painted style with no AI, and an optional download adds an AI photo finish. Regens come from real countries, with a mix of ancestry that follows each nation's footballers.

Aging runs from 15 to 70 at career stages, and weight after football and stress greying mark the face. The top risks are the art job for one reviewer and a photo finish that runs too slowly on real PCs.

Decisions

What realistic means

  • All four Football Manager complaints matter: the plastic look, faces that never age, clones, and faces wrong for the nation.
  • The first version makes faces read as real people and makes every regen look individual. Aging and the fit to a nation must be acceptable, not excellent.
  • The player profile, at about 200 to 300 pixels, sets the detail bar.
  • Every face stores an identity that can age from the first version.Why: faces in a save must never change when aging arrives.

The face genome with an AI finish Area closed · all core · first version

  • The lead identity basis is a face genome on a 3D head with a light AI pass. Licensed AI identity tools, a seed with an age slider, and a model we train are not taken further now.Accepted risk: the most engineering of the four; hair and the licence of the tools that guide the AI pass are unproven.
  • The head model is FLAME 2023 Open for shape, with MakeHuman CC0 age and body targets adapted onto it, in our own code.Accepted risk: fitting the targets onto FLAME is our own work.
  • Age lives in the 3D head, both shape and surface. The AI pass only paints and must not change the age.Top risk: wrinkle and skin sets for every decade across every skin tone.

Hair and beards

  • Hairstyle changes at life moments: a first-team debut, a transfer, turning about 30, retirement, and club milestones. Each regen has his own tendency.
  • Hair and beards are hair cards in 3D, authored per style.
  • Coiled hair gets dedicated authoring: braids and locs as geometry, fades and cornrows as scalp textures.
  • 150 or more hairstyles across all hair types in the first version.Accepted risk: the largest art job.
  • Code builds the structure, studio AI makes the textures, no artists, and you review every result.Accepted risk: one reviewer, and studio AI is weakest on coiled hair and dark skin.

Faces and nations Area closed · all core · first version

  • Regens come from real countries, and all four origin failures are unacceptable: wrong for the nation, one look per nation, no diaspora or mix, coarse skin tone.
  • Each regen carries a mix of ancestry proportions over about 20 regional gene pools.
  • Spreads follow each nation’s footballers: population data, shifted by public birthplace and eligibility records. No faces of real players are used.
  • Heritage also gives a second nation for international football.
  • Family looks for sons, nephews, and brothers of retired players come after the first version.Later.

Player trust Area closed · first version

  • Generative AI is in scope, with the Steam disclosure accepted, including live generation.
  • Studio audits of thousands of sample faces check likeness to real footballers before release.Accepted risk: nothing screens at run time.
  • Open by design: the store, a plain settings text, and a public page on how faces are made. The wording is precise: code decides who a player is and how old he looks, studio AI made the skin and hair materials, and the optional finisher paints light and skin.
  • The painted mode is a look, not an AI choice; the game makes no AI claim about it.
  • No limit is set on the first version.Accepted risk: a large first version for one reviewer.

Aging across a life Area closed · all core · first version

  • Aging runs from 15 to 70, through coaching and management.
  • Faces update at career stages, with two academy stages (15 to 17 and 18 to 20), then breakthrough, peak, veteran, retired, and staff decades.
  • Weight after football and faster greying under pressure mark the face. Injury marks and tattoos are not included.
  • If the art job forces a cut, hair loss moves to later first.

The renderer

  • A separate Rust portrait process renders faces with wgpu and runs the finisher beside it, and hands images to the page.Accepted risk: a third process to ship and supervise.
  • Followed players (squad and shortlist) have a baked current-age face; every other face is drawn on demand and cached.
  • Lists show the render at once, and the finisher upgrades faces in the background.
  • A followed face updates at age-band edges and at life moments.Accepted risk: aging shows as steps between bands.
  • The painted style is a painterly shader with no AI, on every PC.

  • Textures ship as compact data that code expands, which keeps the spirit of the product rule.
  • Generative AI stays in scope.Accepted risk: Steam disclosure, including live generation, and the backlash against AI art.

Ideas still open

  • A hidden style trait sets how often a regen changes his look: a showman often, a quiet professional never.

What the research found

Prior art in games
  • Football Manager added 2D regen faces in 2008 and 3D faces in 2018; players still call them plastic, and no source describes any aging. FullerFM
  • Fan mods assign pre-made GAN photos by player ID from about 14 appearance folders; the photos never age; the tools are GPL-3.0. NewGAN Manager
  • EA FC youth players come from a small head bank; players report clones and faces that never age.
  • Out of the Park Baseball ages FaceGen heads across a career. OOTP manual
  • Crusader Kings 3 stores a DNA string of gene sliders, children inherit genes, and age is a layer over the same head. GDC talk
Licences
  • FLAME 2023 Open allows commercial use under CC-BY-4.0.Checked FLAME licence
  • MakeHuman assets and morph targets are CC0; exports carry no limitation.Checked MakeHuman licence
  • faces.js is Apache-2.0.Checked faces.js
  • StyleGAN weights and the FFHQ face set are non-commercial.
  • FLUX.1 schnell is Apache-2.0; FLUX.1 dev and SDXL-Turbo are non-commercial; SDXL and SDXL-Lightning allow commercial use.
  • Almost every identity tool depends on InsightFace weights, which need a paid commercial licence. InsightFace licensing
  • Most learned aging models build on StyleGAN or non-commercial face sets.
Trust, scale, and hardware
  • Steam asks for disclosure of player-facing AI content; generation during play also needs written guardrails.
  • Games accused of AI art in 2025 and 2026 met review bombs and lost awards.
  • The EU AI Act labelling duty for images that resemble real people started on 2 August 2026. Article 50
  • Diffusion models can reproduce training photos of real people; a similarity check and an exclusion list of real footballers are the defence. Carlini and others, 2023
  • About half of Steam players have a graphics card with 8 GB or more.
  • A finished library of 50,000 faces at four ages is about 16 GB; a genome is a few hundred bytes.
  • Player records today carry no nationality, birth date, or appearance fields.
